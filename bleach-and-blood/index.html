About the game

Night shift horror in a quiet town

Evelyn Miller moved to a quiet town to escape her past. Now she works nights at a laundromat, serving strange customers while unsettling events begin to unfold.

Take orders, wash clothes, explore the store and pass the time between customers. As suspicion falls on someone close to Evelyn, each shift becomes more dangerous.

Retro PS1-inspired visuals

Low-resolution, late-1990s-inspired horror presentation built around atmosphere and unease.

Realistic job gameplay

Serve customers, take orders and handle laundromat tasks throughout the night shift.

Interactive environments

Explore the laundromat and surrounding spaces as the story develops around Evelyn.

Computer minigames

Pass quieter moments of the shift with in-world computer activities before the night turns worse.
