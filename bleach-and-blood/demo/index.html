Where to download

The free demo is available from the game's Steam page and itch.io page. On Steam, open the store page and select the demo download option. On itch.io, open the game's download section and select a build for your operating system.

Demo at a glance

  • Genre: first-person retro slasher horror
  • Estimated playtime: approximately 25–35 minutes, varying by player
  • Language: English
  • itch.io builds: Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Full release: planned for 2027 on Steam

Check each store listing for its current platform availability and system requirements before downloading.

What happens in the demo?

You play as Evelyn Miller, who has moved to a quiet town and taken a job at a laundromat. The demo follows the opening chapter, including Evelyn's first night shift. Everyday tasks and unsettling encounters begin to overlap as the town's ongoing violence comes closer to home.

Expect a mix of exploration, interactions with customers, routine laundromat work and atmospheric story moments. The demo is a work in progress and may differ from the finished game.

Common questions

Is Bleach & Blood free?

The demo is free. The full game is a separate upcoming release; check its Steam store page for availability.

Is the demo the entire game?

No. The demo contains the opening chapter, focused on Evelyn's first shift. The full game is planned for 2027.

Can I play on a Mac or Linux computer?

Downloadable macOS and Linux demo builds are listed on itch.io, alongside Windows. Platform availability may differ between stores and may change with updates.

Does the demo support a controller?

The itch.io listing includes controller inputs. Check the store page for up-to-date controller details and compatibility.
