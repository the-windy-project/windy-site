Official description

Night-shift slasher horror

Bleach & Blood is a story-driven PS1-inspired slasher horror game by The Windy Project. Play as Evelyn Miller, who has moved to a quiet town to escape her past and taken a night-shift job at a small-town laundromat.

Serve customers and complete everyday tasks as strange encounters and unsettling events reveal that the streets outside are no longer safe.

A free demo featuring the opening chapter is available, with the full game planned for Steam in 2027.
