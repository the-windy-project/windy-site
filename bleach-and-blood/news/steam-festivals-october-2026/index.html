What to expect from the demo

Players take the role of Evelyn Miller, who moved to a quieter town hoping to leave her past behind. She now works at a laundromat, handling orders and serving customers while increasingly unsettling events unfold around her.

The demo includes the opening chapter and Evelyn's first night shift. It combines retro graphics, everyday job interactions, exploration and story-driven horror. Estimated playtime is around 25–30 minutes, depending on how you play.
